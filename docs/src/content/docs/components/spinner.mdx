## Notes

**It says something to a screen reader.** A page that is busy is otherwise
silently identical to a page that is empty. The word for what is loading comes
from the product:

```tsx
<Spinner label={t('loading.versions')} />
```

**Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it stops turning.** That is not a detail: for
some readers a spinning thing is not decoration but a symptom. A stopped
spinner still says "busy" through its role, which is the part carrying the
meaning anyway.

| `tone` | `current \| dim \| accent` | `current` | Follows the surrounding text by default |
